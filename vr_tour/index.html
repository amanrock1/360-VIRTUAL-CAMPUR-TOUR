VR mode

View the campus through a Cardboard-style phone headset.

  1. Make sure your phone's rotation lock is off, and allow motion access when asked.
  2. Press Enter VR, then place the phone in the headset in landscape.
  3. Look around by turning your head. To move, look at a round arrow for 1.5 seconds.
  4. Tap the screen or headset button to re-centre the view. Look straight down to exit.
