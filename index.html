About this tour

This virtual tour helps prospective students, parents and visitors see the VIT Bhopal campus before they arrive. Each location was photographed in 360°, so you can look in every direction and move from place to place as if walking through the campus.

A short guided video plays at some locations. For a headset experience, open the tour in VR mode.

How to use the tour

Look around

Drag with your mouse or swipe on a touch screen. Use the + and − buttons to zoom.

Move between places

Click the round arrows in the view to walk to the next location.

Jump to a place

Open the Locations menu at the top left to go straight to any area.

VR mode

Use the VR button in the toolbar and place a phone in a headset such as Google Cardboard.
